## 🚀 Overview
If you want great-looking videos without spending hours on the timeline, CapCut for Windows is built around exactly that. Its standout strength is a rich collection of CapCut templates: pre-designed layouts with timing, effects, and transitions already set, so you simply drop in your own clips and text and let the template do the heavy lifting. That makes it ideal for social content, where speed and following trends matter. But CapCut is more than templates — behind the quick-start workflow sits a capable video editor with a multi-track timeline, filters, music, and captions, so once you've built a video from a template you can keep customizing it as far as you like. It's a fast, flexible way to produce polished videos on your PC.

## ✨ Features
- **Huge template library** — browse and apply CapCut templates to create polished, trend-following videos in minutes.
- **Drop-in editing** — replace a template's placeholders with your own clips, photos, and text without rebuilding from scratch.
- **Effects and filters** — layer transitions, filters, and visual effects to make each video stand out.
- **Music and captions** — add soundtracks and animated text to set the mood and get your message across.
- **Customizable timeline** — go beyond templates with full multi-track editing whenever you want more control.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open CapCut on your Windows PC and browse the template library.
2. Pick a CapCut template that matches the style of video you want.
3. Import your clips and photos, then drop them into the template's placeholders.
4. Adjust the text, music, and effects to make the video your own.
5. Preview the result and export it at your chosen resolution.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is CapCut free?**
Yes. CapCut is free to download, and its template library and core editing tools are available at no cost. A CapCut Pro option adds extra features and premium content.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
CapCut runs on 64-bit Windows, primarily Windows 10 and Windows 11. A currently supported version is recommended.

**How do CapCut templates work?**
Each template comes with preset timing, effects, and transitions. You replace the placeholder clips with your own footage and text, and CapCut assembles a finished, trend-ready video.

**Is my data secure?**
CapCut lets you edit on your own Windows PC. Use a trusted account to sign in, keep the app updated, and handle your exported videos like any other files on your computer.
